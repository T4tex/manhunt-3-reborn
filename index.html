About the game

Hunted. Cornered. Relentless.

MANHUNT 3 - Reborn is a fan-made survival horror experience inspired by the tension, dread, and panic of urban pursuit. Every route matters, every shadow feels alive, and every mistake can turn the city against you.

Built for players who crave cinematic stealth, nerve-wracking chases, and an oppressive atmosphere, this project channels the spirit of classic pursuit horror into a fresh, modern showcase.
